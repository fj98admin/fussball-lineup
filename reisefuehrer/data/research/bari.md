## 1. PORT LOGISTICS

**Terminal:** Nuova Stazione Marittima (Bari Cruise Terminal), Molo San Vito. Modern terminal, no ATM inside (nearest just outside the gate — Banco Popolare di Bari ~2min walk).

**To Bari Vecchia:** ~1–2.5km / 20–35min walk. Route: through the port/industrial stretch, then join the **Lungomare** for a nicer final approach. Practical route: exit gate, first left onto Corso Antonio De Tullio, then left again into the old town.

**Shuttle:** Some lines shuttle to Piazza Aldo Moro (train station hub); unconfirmed for this specific TUI call — check daily program. Taxis available (~10min to old town). Public bus Line 6 reportedly connects port↔Piazza Aldo Moro.

**Orientation:** Bari Vecchia is a dense medieval knot — download offline maps first. Two "faces": harbor/fish-market side (west) and Lungomare Nazario Sauro seafront (facing the ship channel). South of old town: the elegant 19th-c. "Murat"/Umbertino grid (Teatro Petruzzelli, boulevards). Suggested loop: port → Lungomare → Castello Svevo → Bari Vecchia alleys/Arco Basso/San Nicola/Cathedral → Piazza Mercantile → Murat district → back to port.

## 2. TOP HIGHLIGHTS

1. **Basilica di San Nicola** — Romanesque fortress-church (begun 1087), holds St. Nicholas relics, rare Orthodox chapel inside. Best light: morning. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Basilica+San+Nicola+Bari
2. **Cattedrale di San Sabino** — 12th-c. Romanesque, more intimate than San Nicola, blind arcading + rear "trulla". Best: golden hour. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cattedrale+di+San+Sabino+Bari
3. **Castello Svevo** — built 1131 (Roger II), expanded 1223+ (Frederick II). Moat, plaster-cast collection, sea-view ramparts. ~€8–10 entry. Best: late afternoon. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Castello+Svevo+Bari
4. **Strada Arco Basso ("Via delle Orecchiette")** — nonne hand-roll fresh orecchiette on street tables, sell by the kilo (~€5/kg). Active ~8:00–12:00 (often gone by 10-noon). Always buy something if photographing; ask permission for close-ups. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Strada+Arco+Basso+Bari
5. **Lungomare Nazario Sauro** — >1km Belle Époque seafront. Best shot from the east where the castle swings into frame. Best: sunset. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lungomare+Nazario+Sauro+Bari
6. **Teatro Petruzzelli** — Italy's 4th-largest opera house (1903, rebuilt after 1991 arson fire, reopened 2009). Best: daytime for facade. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Teatro+Petruzzelli+Bari
7. **Piazza Mercantile** — historic market square, porticoes, "Colonna della Giustizia". Best: aperitivo hour 18:00+. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Piazza+Mercantile+Bari
8. **Fishing harbor / Molo San Nicola** — small painted boats, fishermen unload/sell catch each morning. Best: 7:00–10:00. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Molo+San+Nicola+Bari+fish+market

## 4. MUST-EAT GUIDE (flagship food chapter)

**Focaccia Barese** — thick olive-oil focaccia, crushed tomato/olives/oregano, crispy-bottom/pillowy inside. **Panificio Fiore** (Strada Palazzo di Città 38, since 1508, "temple of focaccia barese", 4.5★ Tripadvisor) — sells out by ~12:00 and ~19:00, go 7:30–10:00. ~€3–4/kg, hand slice ~€1–1.50. Eat standing on the street.

**Orecchiette** — hand-shaped "little ears," classic with cime di rapa (turnip tops) or ragù. Watch made fresh at Strada Arco Basso (~€5/kg to buy). Eat cooked at: **Le Arpie** (best in old town, book ahead), **Antò** (family-run, ~200m from San Nicola), **La Uascezze**, **Orecchietteria San Nicola** (with braciole), **La Cantina dello Zio** (paired with Primitivo). Pasta main: €8–14.

**Burrata** — actually from **Andria** (~1hr inland), invented 1956 by Lorenzo Bianchino, protected as Burrata di Andria IGP. Ask for "di giornata" (of the day) at any old-town salumeria/trattoria. €6–12 starter. Eat within hours if bought to-go.

**Panzerotti** — fried half-moon dough, mozzarella+tomato classic. **Panzeropoli by Benny** (Piazza Mercantile 35, ~17 varieties), **Mastrociccio** (Piazza Mercantile), **Nonno Marcello** (Via Fanelli), **Pizzeria Di Cosimo**. €3–6.50 each. Eat immediately — molten center is scalding.

**Gelato** — **Gelateria Gentile** (since 1880, historic institution) or **Caruso Gelateria** (Piazza Garibaldi 22, since 1956). €2.50–4. Try fior di latte or fig/almond seasonal.

**Taralli** — ring crackers, flour/olive oil/white wine, plain+fennel classic. Any panificio/alimentari. €3–6/bag — good edible souvenir.

**Sgagliozze (fried polenta)** — golden fried polenta squares, salted, cucina povera street food. **Maria delle Sgagliozze** fries on her doorstep from ~18:00 (evening snack, not morning); also around Piazza Mercantile. €1–3/bag.

**Raw seafood / "crudo di mare"** — Bari's defining food tradition, eating urchins/oysters/mussels/octopus raw straight off the boats (regulated since the 1500s). Morning-only (Section 5). Genuine risk-tolerance food experience — be honest about that trade-off.

**Puglia wine** — Primitivo (Zinfandel-related, full-bodied; look for Primitivo di Manduria/Gioia del Colle) and Negroamaro (Salento, dark fruit/tobacco/bitter-almond finish). **Mostofiore** wine bar recommended. Glass €4–7, bottle from €15+.

**Espresso** — standard bar-counter culture, ~€1–1.50 standing. Caffè Leccese (iced espresso + almond milk) is technically a Lecce specialty but available in Bari too — good hot-day option.

## 5. STREET FOOD MARKETS

**Harbor Fish Market ("Nderr a la Lanz")** — informal open-air spectacle on Molo San Nicola near old Teatro Margherita; fishermen lay out catch (octopus, scallops, oysters, mussels, razor clams, sardines, urchins, vongole) on tables/crates. Eat crudo di mare here. **Best: 7:00–12:00**, packs up early afternoon — make it one of the first stops.

**Piazza Mercantile & alleys** — evening street-food/aperitivo cluster (panzerotti, sgagliozze vendors, bars) rather than a market — treat as an evening food-crawl hub.

## 6. TOP 5 RESTAURANTS

1. **Best value:** Antò — old town, ~200m from San Nicola, home-style, orecchiette-focused, mains ~€8–14.
2. **Upscale:** Le Arpie — old town, one of Bari's best, refined orecchiette/seafood, reserve a day ahead, mains ~€18–28+.
3. **Local secret:** Orecchietteria San Nicola — casual basement under stone vaults near the basilica.
4. **Best rooftop/view:** Sparano Palace Cafe & Rooftop; also a wine bar along the old city walls near Fortino Sant'Antonio for sunset drinks.
5. **Best cocktail bar:** Dilman Lounge Bistrot, POP Bottega Urbana (trendy, high TheFork ratings); Mostofiore for low-key natural wine instead.

**Breakfast:** no standout single venue — bar-counter espresso+cornetto anywhere, or better, swap for a crudo di mare seafood "breakfast" at the harbor.

## 9. PRACTICAL

- **ATM:** none in terminal; Banco Popolare di Bari ~2min walk from port gate; common in old town/Murat.
- **Pharmacy/supermarket:** not confirmed at fixed address near terminal — use live "pharmacy near me" search; likely along Corso Antonio De Tullio or in Bari Vecchia.
- **Safety:** reasonably safe overall; pickpocketing is the main issue (crowded Bari Vecchia pockets, Piazza Aldo Moro/station area, buses). Standard precautions (cross-body bag, front pocket, don't leave phone on café tables). Daytime old-town wandering low-risk; no active-threat warnings for Aug 2026.
- **Solo social spot:** small-group tours (food/bike/pasta) are the easiest natural socializing point; also wine bars (Mostofiore), cocktail spots, and the Piazza Mercantile aperitivo scene.
