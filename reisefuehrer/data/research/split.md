## 1. PORT LOGISTICS

**Docking:** Directly on the city harbor, adjacent to Old Town, two berth zones:
- **Gat Sv. Petra (inner):** ~5min/~490m to the Silver Gate. **Note:** multi-year reconstruction works may redirect 2026–2027 calls to outer berths — confirm on daily program.
- **Gat Svetog Duje (outer):** used increasingly by larger ships. 15–20min walk along the harbor road/Riva — flat, scenic, no shuttle needed.

Either way, Diocletian's Palace is max ~1km/13min walk — one of the most walkable cruise ports in the Mediterranean.

**Orientation:** Walk the Riva east/southeast to reach the Old Town's harbor-side entrance. Compact walkable maze — every alley leads back to the Peristyle or Riva. Ferry terminal/bus hub/taxis cluster at the port; day-tour meeting points (rafting/zipline/Blue Lagoon) typically collect near the "SPLIT" sign/bus station by the port.

⚠️ Exact port hours for the specific Aug 2026 call unconfirmed — verify onboard (typically ~08:00–17:00/18:00 on Adriatic itineraries).

## 2. TOP HIGHLIGHTS

1. **Diocletian's Palace (UNESCO)** — built ~295–305 AD as Diocletian's retirement palace, evolved into a living neighborhood inside the Roman walls. Best: 07:00–09:00 or after 19:00. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Diocletian%27s+Palace+Split
2. **Peristyle (Peristil)** — monumental central courtyard, red Egyptian granite columns, black granite sphinxes, cathedral bell tower above. Best: morning or lit evening. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Peristil+Split
3. **Cathedral of St. Domnius + Bell Tower** — Diocletian's former mausoleum, arguably oldest Catholic cathedral still in original structure; ~200 steps up the 13th-c. tower. Ticket ~€10 (or ~€11 combined). Hours Mon–Sat 08–20, Sun 12–18 (verify). Best 360° viewpoint in the city — go at opening or ~1h before closing. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cathedral+of+Saint+Domnius+Split
4. **Riva Promenade** — palm-lined waterfront, Split's living room. Best: early morning (fishermen, quiet) or sunset/blue hour. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Riva+Split+Croatia
5. **Marjan Hill Forest Park** — pine peninsula park, Old Jewish Cemetery (1573), hermitage chapels. Viewpoints: **Vidilica** (15–20min up from Veli Varoš, café terrace) and **Telegrin** (178m, highest, giant flag + stone cross). Best: golden hour before sunset (faces city to the east). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Marjan+Hill+Vidilica+Split
6. **Bačvice Beach** — Split's sandy city beach, birthplace of picigin (water-volleyball game, UNESCO-nominated). Beach bar/club strip by night. Best: early morning (calm/glow) or evening (atmosphere). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bacvice+Beach+Split
7. **Fish Market (Ribarnica)** — Art Nouveau market hall, 120+ years, fish sold here since ≥1312. Local lore: legendarily free of smell/flies due to sulphur spring beneath Old Town. Best: before 09:00. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fish+Market+Ribarnica+Split
8. **Golden Gate + Grgur Ninski statue** — grandest palace gate; Meštrović's 8.5m bronze statue outside, toe rubbed golden for luck. Best: morning. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Golden+Gate+Diocletian%27s+Palace+Split

**Bonus:** Diocletian's Palace Substructures/Cellars — GoT filming location (Daenerys's throne room), central nave free, side halls ~€5–10. Cool, atmospheric.

## 4. MUST-EAT DALMATIAN SPECIALTIES

- **Soparnik** — thin unleavened pie, Swiss chard/onion/garlic/olive oil, baked under embers. EU PGI status. €3–4/slice. **Pazar (Green Market)**, sold by older women before 11:00, best warm straight from market.
- **Pašticada** — festive dish, beef marinated up to 24h then slow-braised with prunes/root veg/prosciutto, sweet-sour sauce, over gnocchi. €20–28 at a proper konoba (€12–15 simpler taverns). Konoba Fetivi (Michelin Bib Gourmand), Konoba Varoš, Tavern Pimpinella, Konoba Stare Grede.
- **Fresh grilled fish** — brancin/orada with blitva (chard+potato). Priced by weight, ~€40–60/kg whole; fillets €18–30. Konoba Nikola, Veli Varoš waterfront konobas. Ask what was caught that morning.
- **Dalmatian pršut & cheese** (often Pag island sheep cheese) — sharing board €12–18. Any konoba as starter.
- **Fritule** — fried dough balls, citrus/rum/raisins, sugar-dusted. €3–5. Bakeries/dessert cafés, best warm.
- **Wine** — Plavac Mali (bold red, Zinfandel-related), Pošip/Grk (crisp island whites). Glass €5–8, bottle €15–35 (Hvar/Vis/Pelješac producers).
- **Burek** — filo pastry, meat/cheese/spinach. €2.50–4. Bakeries, best hot morning with plain yogurt.

## 5. TOP RESTAURANTS

- **Best value:** Konoba Stare Grede / Tavern Tri Volta — traditional dishes, mains from ~€12.
- **Upscale:** Storija Fine Dining (inside the palace walls) or Restaurant Dvor / Restaurant Sug (Michelin Guide-recognized) — €50–100+/person.
- **Local secret:** Konoba Fetivi (Veli Varoš, Michelin Bib Gourmand, daily-changing Pazar-market menu); also Villa Spiza.
- **Best breakfast/coffee:** KaKantun Specialty Coffee & Gin (alley inside the Palace) or The Daltonist.
- **Best beach bar (Bačvice):** Tropic Club — tiki/tropical open-air, free entry, busy evening into late night in summer — THE social scene for a solo traveler.
- **Best gelato:** Ela's Gelateria (4.9★, Old Town) or Gelateria Emiliana.

## 6. TOP 3 ACTIVITIES (with timing-feasibility check)

### 🥇 Cetina River Rafting (near Omiš, ~25km/40–50min drive)
Grade II–III rapids, dramatic green canyon, beginner-suitable, swim/cliff-jump stops. GYG listings: "From Split/Šestanovac: Whitewater Rafting" (4.5–6h, ~$42/~€38–40, 4.8★), "Cetina River: Rafting, Cliff Jumping and Cave Exploring" (3–6h, ~$52, 4.9★), "Split/Omiš: Rafting with Cliff Jump & Swimming" (4–6h, ~$57, 4.9★). Meeting point: red "SPLIT" sign near bus station, close to cruise port.
**⚠️ TIMING RISK:** Total ~4.5–6h door-to-door (transfer ~1.5–2h + rafting ~2–2.5h). Feasible only if port day is a full ~9–10h call and you book earliest morning slot + shortest (3–4h) version + 90min minimum slack before all-aboard. Highest timing risk of the three activities.

### 🥈 Zipline Croatia — Cetina Canyon (near Omiš)
8-line circuit, first line ~700m at ~150m above canyon floor, ~2,100m total. "Omiš: 2.5-Hour Cetina Canyon ZIPLINE" — ~$91–92 (~€80–85), 4.9★ (1,200+ reviews). Base ~€80 self-drive, ~€100 with Split transfer. Total ~4–5h door-to-door — moderate timing risk, somewhat more efficient than rafting.

### 🥉 Blue Lagoon Boat Trip (Krknjaši Bay, near Trogir/Šolta)
Turquoise shallow bay, ~12nm from Split, often combined with Trogir (UNESCO) + a third island. GYG: "Split: Small-Group Blue Lagoon and 3 Islands Speedboat Tour" (5h), similar listings 5–5.5h. Viator ~$58–105 (~€53–95). **Lowest timing risk** — departs directly from Split harbor, no road transfer.

**Recommendation:** generous port hours (9+h) → rafting is the top adrenaline pick; shorter/uncertain hours → Blue Lagoon is safest timing-wise, zipline in between.

## 8. PRACTICAL

- **Pharmacy:** Pharmacy Matejuška, by the Riva/small fishing port — English-speaking staff.
- **ATM:** Riva promenade and Marmontova street; also near Trg Republike/Fish Market.
- **Supermarket:** Studenac/Konzum/Tommy chains scattered in Old Town alleys; larger stores along Marmontova.
- **Safety:** Very safe overall; petty crime/pickpocketing reportedly up in 2026 in crowded zones (Riva, Old Town alleys, bus/ferry station) — standard precautions sufficient.
- **Solo social spot:** Bačvice Beach — join picigin by day, Tropic Club and beach bars by night, naturally mixes locals/travelers. Loud/lively from ~22:00 in August.
