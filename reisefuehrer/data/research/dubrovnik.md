## 1. PORT LOGISTICS

**Docking:** Port of **Gruž**, ~2.5–3km NW of the Old Town (large ships dock, don't tender).

**Gruž → Old Town:**
- Ship shuttle bus to/near Pile Gate (~10–15min; up to ~€15 return if charged — check daily program).
- Public bus 1A/1B (also 1, 1C, 8) Gruž→Pile Gate, ~€2–2.50 one-way, 10–20min — best value.
- Walk: 2.5–3km, 30–40min along the harbor road.
- Taxi: ~€15–20 to Pile Gate.

**Cruise crowd caps (important):** Since 2018, Dubrovnik caps cruise traffic ("Respect the City"): max 2 large ships/day + 1 smaller vessel, targeting ~8,000 disembarking pax on busiest days. Ships must now stay longer (min 8–12h) instead of old 4h calls, spreading crowds through the day. **From 2026, City Walls require advance/timed booking**, coach groups get assigned Pile Gate drop-off slots.

**Practical takeaway:** Even with caps, peak August + non-cruise tourists mean Stradun/Walls are packed 10:00–17:00. Being at Pile Gate for wall opening (08:00) is the single most important crowd-avoidance move.

**Cable car to Mt Srđ:** Lower station ~10–15min uphill walk from Pile Gate (not walkable directly from port). Operates ~09:00–21:00 high season (verify). Return ticket ~€30 adult (~€17 child), +€2 online booking surcharge. Last cabin 30min before closing.

## 2. TOP HIGHLIGHTS

1. **City Walls Walk** — 2km circuit, 13th–16th c. fortifications, 1–2h. Best at exactly 08:00 opening (empty + cool); by 10:00 shoulder-to-shoulder, zero shade. Ticket ~€35–40, **2026 requires advance timed booking**. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Walls+of+Dubrovnik+Pile+Gate
2. **Stradun (Placa)** — 300m polished-limestone promenade, baroque facades, Franciscan Monastery/Old Pharmacy, clock tower/Luža Square. Golden hour for warm stone glow; crowd crush midday. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Stradun+Dubrovnik
3. **Fort Lovrijenac** — clifftop fortress (37m), the Red Keep in Game of Thrones. Best shot from Buža cliffs or kayak/boat, not from inside. ~€15 standalone, free with Walls ticket (3-day validity). Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Fort+Lovrijenac+Dubrovnik
4. **Rector's Palace** — Gothic-Renaissance museum, good shaded midday option. ~€15, 09:00–18:00 summer. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Rector%27s+Palace+Dubrovnik
5. **Fort St. John & Aquarium** — guards Old Port entrance, small Adriatic aquarium + Maritime Museum. ~€8–20, ~10:00–19:00, closed Mon. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Fort+St+John+Dubrovnik
6. **Mt Srđ viewpoint** — 412m summit, whole peninsula + Elaphiti Islands. Best sunset spot near Dubrovnik; arrive ~90min early. Return ticket ~€30. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Mount+Srd+Dubrovnik
7. **Lokrum Island** — 15min ferry, botanical garden, "Dead Sea" salt lake, GoT "Iron Throne" replica, swimming spots. Combined ferry+entry ~€30. Watch last-boat time. Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Lokrum+Island
8. **Buža Bar / Buža Gate + GoT walk** — cliffside bars over the Adriatic, cash only, cliff-jumping culture — Dubrovnik's most photogenic hangout. Combine with free GoT self-guided walk (Pile Gate, Jesuit Staircase, Lovrijenac exterior, Minčeta Tower). Maps: https://maps.google.com/?q=Buza+Bar+Dubrovnik

## 4. MUST-EAT DALMATIAN SPECIALTIES

- **Crni rižot (black risotto)** — cuttlefish-ink risotto. €18–25. Konoba Dubrava / Proto, with Pošip.
- **Peka** — meat/octopus + veg slow-cooked under an iron bell — **order 24–48h ahead**. €25–35/portion (serves 2+).
- **Fresh grilled fish (branzino/orata)** — priced by weight, €60–90+/kg, often split for two.
- **Dalmatian pršut & sir** (incl. Paški sir) — sharing board €12–18.
- **Rozata** — Dubrovnik's rose-liqueur custard dessert. €6–8.
- **Wine — Pošip (white, Korčula) & Plavac Mali (red, Pelješac)** — ~€6–10/glass, €25–45/bottle (estimate).
- **Šporki makaruli ("dirty macaroni")** — Dubrovnik's own beef-ragù pasta, historically tied to Feast of St. Blaise. €14–18, less touristy — seek it out.

Pricing reality: bakery/street food €7–10; mid-range konoba €20–35; dinner for two w/ wine €60–90; fine dining €150–200+ for two. Streets inland from the walls run roughly half the Stradun-front price.

## 5. TOP RESTAURANTS

- **Value/mid-range:** Dalmatino (near Gundulić Square) — seafood, good tuna steak.
- **Upscale:** Restaurant 360 (Michelin-starred, built into the walls, Adriatic-French, harbor views); also Nautika, Pjerin (Villa Dubrovnik). €100+/person.
- **Local secret:** Konoba Dubrava — residential north Old Town, octopus peka, black risotto, honester prices.
- **Breakfast/coffee:** Cogito Coffee Shop (2 locations, rated Dubrovnik's best); Orlando Café; Dubravka 1836 near Pile Gate (scenic terrace).
- **Rooftop/cliffside bar:** Buža Bar / Buža II (cash only, cliff-jump scene); Love Bar (rooftop, affordable, local crowd); Cave Bar More (in an actual sea cave, polished cocktails).

Reverify Michelin status close to trip date (annual awards).

## 6. TOP 3 ACTIVITIES

### 🥇 Kajak — Sea kayaking around the walls & Lokrum
Depart Pile Bay, paddle beneath the walls/Lovrijenac, swim/snorkel at Betina Cave, loop to Lokrum. Daytime ~2–2.5h (~4km) **€32–35**; 3h sunset tour (~7.5km, incl. wine) **€40–45**; full-day Elaphiti trips **€70–100+**. GetYourGuide/Viator + operators like Adventure Dubrovnik (multi-year TripAdvisor "Best of the Best"). GYG listing: https://www.getyourguide.com/dubrovnik-l513/dubrovnik-sea-kayakingsnorkelling-guided-daysunset-tours-t146145/
*Why #1:* unique low-angle wall/Lovrijenac shot, active, cools off in heat, feels adventurous not touristy.

### 🥈 Speedboot — Elaphiti Islands & Blue Cave
"From Dubrovnik: Elaphiti Islands & Blue Cave Speedboat Tour" (~4h) from **~$89**; "Full-Day Blue Cave & Elaphiti Islands" (8h, 4.8★/719 reviews) **$57–74**; private charter (up to 6–7 people, 6h) **~€570–630** total. Stops: Blue Cave (Koločep), Lopud/Šunj Beach, sometimes Šipan. Note: fuel surcharge (~€60–150) often added on board — flag to traveler.

### 🥉 Stadtmauer — City Walls self-guided
~€35–40, 1–2h, **2026 requires advance timed booking** (wallsofdubrovnik.com / ulaznice.hr or GYG/Viator skip-the-line). Non-negotiable must-do — ranked 3rd only because it's a walk not an adrenaline activity; schedule at 08:00 regardless of other bookings.

**Kayak comparison (Dubrovnik vs Kotor):** Dubrovnik = open-Adriatic wall-hugging route, cave stop, Lokrum loop, premium/iconic sunset product, more physically demanding swell. Kotor = enclosed fjord-like bay, calmer/flatter water, dramatic mountain scenery, cheaper (~35% per general comparisons), better for beginners/scenery-over-adrenaline.

## 8. PRACTICAL

- **Pharmacy/ATM:** Old Pharmacy (1317, working chemist + museum) just inside Pile Gate on Stradun. Modern pharmacies/ATMs cluster at Pile Gate and along Stradun — prefer bank-wall ATMs over freestanding Euronet-style machines (poor rates/fees).
- **Near port (Gruž):** Konzum supermarket + pharmacies/ATMs near the Gruž bus terminal/market — cheaper than Old Town prices.
- **Safety:** Very safe overall; main risk is pickpocketing in crowd bottlenecks (Stradun, Pile Gate queue, Walls entrance, cable car queue, buses, port).
- **Solo social spot:** Buža Bar cliffside scene (younger/backpacker vibe despite upscale city reputation); Banje Beach bars near Ploče Gate.
